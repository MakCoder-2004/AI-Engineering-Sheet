# AI Engineering Study Guide

A comprehensive collection of study notes covering the full spectrum of AI engineering, from transformer fundamentals to production deployment of multi-agent systems.

## Study Path

The material is organized into 12 topic-based sections, designed to be followed sequentially:

### 1. Foundations

> [01.Foundations/](01.Foundations/)

Transformer architectures, attention mechanisms, encoder/decoder models, BERT, GPT, and the fundamentals behind large language models.

### 2. LLM APIs

> [02.LLM_APIs/](02.LLM_APIs/)

Working with LLM APIs (OpenAI), sending requests, managing costs, and building multi-turn conversations.

### 3. Prompt Engineering

> [03.Prompt_Engineering/](03.Prompt_Engineering/)

Structuring effective prompts, advanced strategies (few-shot, chain-of-thought), business applications, and chatbot development.

### 4. Hugging Face Ecosystem

> [04.Hugging_Face_Ecosystem/](04.Hugging_Face_Ecosystem/)

Building pipelines, working with datasets, model search, tokenizers, and preprocessing with the Hugging Face library.

### 5. Fine-Tuning

> [05.Fine_Tuning/](05.Fine_Tuning/)

Fine-tuning pre-trained LLMs, custom training loops, and evaluation of fine-tuned models.

### 6. Computer Vision

> [06.Computer_Vision/](06.Computer_Vision/)

Image understanding, Vision Transformers (ViT), image classification, and fine-tuning vision models.

### 7. Speech and Audio

> [07.Speech_and_Audio/](07.Speech_and_Audio/)

Automatic speech recognition (ASR), text-to-speech (TTS), audio classification, and music generation with models like Whisper.

### 8. Multi-Modal AI

> [08.Multi_Modal_AI/](08.Multi_Modal_AI/)

Multi-modal classification with CLIP, image-text matching, Stable Diffusion, and text-to-image generation.

### 9. Evaluation and Metrics

> [09.Evaluation_and_Metrics/](09.Evaluation_and_Metrics/)

BLEU, ROUGE, BERTScore, perplexity, and comprehensive evaluation methods for language tasks.

### 10. AI Agents and RAG

> [10.AI_Agents_and_RAG/](10.AI_Agents_and_RAG/)

Building agents with smolagents, agentic RAG, multi-agent orchestration, memory management, validation, and production agent systems.

### 11. Production Systems

> [11.Production_Systems/](11.Production_Systems/)

System architecture, deployment strategies, scaling, monitoring, and running AI systems in production.

### 12. Projects

> [Projects/](Projects/)

End-to-end projects applying concepts from all sections, including car review analysis with LLMs.
